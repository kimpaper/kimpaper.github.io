October 2026 festivals outside the Seoul capital area, checked as of October 12. The big first-half festivals (Andong Mask Dance, Geumsan Ginseng, Baekje Cultural Festival) are over, so this guide covers what is still ahead. For Seoul, Gyeonggi and Incheon, see the October capital-area post.

Dates come from official sites, city and county notices and 2026 news. Regional festivals usually run free shuttles from stations or remote parking, so shuttle details are included where published. Photos are freely licensed pictures of past festivals or the venues.

Gangwon

Gangneung Coffee Festival (강릉커피축제)

Coffee cup sculpture at Anmok Beach · Photo: Mobius6, 2022 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 21–25, 10:00–20:00
  • Where: Gangneung Coffee Street at Anmok Beach, plus venues around the city
  • Fee: free
  • Getting there: KTX from Seoul to Gangneung (about 2 hours), then bus or taxi; parking near the beach fills up
  • Official: https://www.coffeefestival.net

Highlights

  • Seaside café strip at Anmok Beach, Korea’s unofficial coffee capital
  • Bread festival at the old government office, Oct 22–25

Chuncheon Makguksu & Dakgalbi Festival (춘천막국수닭갈비축제)

Chuncheon dakgalbi · Photo: Fumikas Sagisavas, 2025 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 14–18, 11:00–21:00
  • Where: Gongjicheon stream walk, Chuncheon

Highlights

  • Dakgalbi (spicy stir-fried chicken on an iron pan), 200 g for ₩9,900
  • Makguksu (cold buckwheat noodles), ₩6,900

Gangneung Noodle Festival (강릉 국수축제)

Jang-kalguksu · Photo: Mobius6, 2022 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 15–18
  • Where: Wolhwa-geori walkway, walking distance from Gangneung station

Highlights

  • Jang-kalguksu, Gangneung’s spicy knife-cut noodle soup

Yangyang Songi & Salmon Festival (양양송이연어축제)

  • When: Oct 16–18
  • Where: Namdaecheon riverbank, Yangyang

Highlights

  • Bare-handed salmon catch as salmon return upriver
  • Pine mushroom (songi) treasure hunt and sales

Chungcheong and Daejeon

Ganggyeong Jeotgal Festival (강경젓갈축제)

Jeotgal for sale · Photo: hwayoungjung, 2007 · CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 15–18, 10:00–22:00
  • Where: Geumgang riverside, Ganggyeong (Ganggyeong station, Honam line)
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Streets of jeotgal (salted fermented seafood) shops, busy before kimchi season
  • Kimchi-making event and riverside market

Yeongdong Nangye Gugak Festival (영동난계국악축제)

  • When: Oct 15–18, 10:00–20:00
  • Where: Rainbow Healing Plaza, Yeongdong
  • Fee: free; some workshops ₩5,000–15,000

Highlights

  • Traditional and fusion gugak (Korean traditional music)
  • Instrument-making workshops

Boeun Jujube Festival (보은대추축제)

Jujubes · Photo: Choe Yongwoo, 2018 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 16–25
  • Where: Baetdeul Park, Boeun, near Songnisan National Park
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Fresh jujubes from local farms
  • Autumn foliage at Songnisan and Beopjusa Temple

Daejeon Bread Festival (대전 빵축제)

Sungsimdang main store, Daejeon · Photo: Bongsun, 2020 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 17–18, 13:00–21:00
  • Where: Hanbit Tower, Expo Science Park
  • Fee: free entry

Highlights

  • 120 bakeries from a city known for its bakeries; go early for the popular ones

Yuseong Chrysanthemum Festival (유성국화축제)

Flower garden at Yurim Park · Photo: UniCon, 2022 · CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 17–Nov 1, 10:00–22:00
  • Where: Yurim Park, Daejeon
  • Fee: free

Jeolla and Gwangju

Jeongeup Gujeolcho Flower Festival (정읍 구절초꽃축제)

Gujeolcho (Korean mountain chrysanthemum) · Photo: 최옥석, 2017 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: until Oct 18
  • Where: Gujeolcho Garden, Sannae-myeon, Jeongeup
  • Fee: ₩7,000 (₩4,000 back as a garden voucher)
  • Getting there: free shuttle from Sannae-myeon, 09:00–18:30

Highlights

  • White wild chrysanthemums (gujeolcho) under a pine forest
  • Suspension bridge, waterfall, flower-field concerts

Sunchang Jang Festival (순창장류축제)

  • When: Oct 15–18
  • Where: Sunchang Traditional Gochujang Village
  • Getting there: shuttles every 20 minutes, 09:00–22:00

Highlights

  • Make your own gochujang
  • Royal tribute procession, tightrope walking

Gochang Moyangseong Festival (고창모양성제)

Gochang Eupseong Fortress · Photo: Lance Vanlewen, 2018 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 15–19
  • Where: Gochang Eupseong fortress

Highlights

  • Dapseong: walking the fortress wall with a stone on your head
  • Drone show Oct 16 at 19:30; Black Eagles air show Oct 17 at 16:00

Mokpo Harbor Festival (목포항구축제)

Mokpo coast · Photo: A Fun Couple, 2021 · CC BY 2.0, Flickr

  • When: Oct 16–18, 12:00–21:00
  • Where: Mokpo port and Samhakdo
  • Getting there: three free shuttle routes

Highlights

  • Re-created floating fish market (pasi), shows at 18:00 on Oct 16–17
  • Fermented skate, croaker, rockfish

Hwasun Dolmen Autumn Flower Festival (화순 고인돌 가을꽃축제)

  • When: Oct 16–25, 09:00–18:00
  • Where: Hwasun Dolmen Site (UNESCO World Heritage)
  • Fee: free on Oct 16; ₩5,000 from Oct 17, refunded as local vouchers

Gwangju Kimchi Festival (광주김치축제)

Gwangju Kimchi Festival · Photo: 준태쨔응, 2016 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 23–25
  • Where: Gwangju government building (former city hall)
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Kimchi-making sessions and sales
  • A thousand-person communal table

Namdo Food Culture Festival (남도음식문화큰잔치)

  • When: Oct 23–25
  • Where: Yeongsan River Garden, Naju

Highlights

  • Food from all 27 cities and counties of the southwest
  • Evening chef shows, including temple-food chef Jeong Kwan on Oct 24

Hampyeong Chrysanthemum Festival (함평 국향대전)

  • When: Oct 23–Nov 8, 09:00–18:00
  • Where: Hampyeong Expo Park
  • Fee: paid; advance tickets discounted until Oct 22

Gyeongsang, Busan, Daegu

Jinju Namgang Yudeung (Lantern) Festival (진주남강유등축제)

The Namgang River during the 2022 lantern festival · Photo: Bernard Gagnon, 2022 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: until Oct 18; lanterns lit 18:00–24:00
  • Where: Jinjuseong Fortress and the Namgang River
  • Fee: free; floating bridge and lantern activities paid
  • Getting there: heavy traffic on Fridays and weekends; use the free shuttles from remote parking
  • Official: http://www.yudeung.com

Highlights

  • Giant lanterns on the river
  • Floating bridge crossing
  • Last fireworks on Oct 17 after a drone show

Bonghwa Songi Festival (봉화송이축제)

Braised pine mushrooms (songi-jorim) · Photo: Eugene Kim, 2010 · CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 15–18
  • Where: Naeseongcheon riverside, Bonghwa
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Pine mushroom sales and three auctions a day
  • Pine-mushroom ramyeon, ₩5,000

Mungyeong Apple Festival (문경사과축제)

Entrance area of Mungyeong Saejae · Photo: P.YEJI, 2023 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: opens Oct 17
  • Where: Mungyeongsaejae Provincial Park

Highlights

  • Apple picking and auctions
  • The old Mungyeongsaejae mountain pass trail

Gimcheon Gimbap Festival (김천김밥축제)

Gimbap · Photo: JaredMcKenzie, 2026 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 23–25
  • Where: Jikji Culture Park, Gimcheon
  • Fee: free

About 80 gimbap and snack stalls. Last year’s crowds jammed the roads, so go early.

Masan Gagopa Chrysanthemum Festival (마산가고파국화축제)

Masan Gagopa Chrysanthemum Festival · Photo: Shinfull, 2017 · CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 31–Nov 8, 09:00–21:00
  • Where: 3·15 Marine Nuri Park, Changwon
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Chrysanthemum displays and a fire-falling show over the sea (new this year)
  • Odong-dong agujjim (spicy braised monkfish) street, 10 minutes away

Busan International Film Festival (부산국제영화제)

Busan Cinema Center during BIFF 2023 · Photo: 399scout, 2023 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: until Oct 15
  • Where: Busan Cinema Center (Centum City station, Line 2)

The Busan Fireworks Festival is on November 7 this year.

Jeju

Tamna Cultural Festival (탐라문화제)

  • When: Oct 17–21
  • Where: Jeju Citizens’ Welfare Town Plaza
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Parade, folk games, dance drama (Oct 18–20, 20:00)

The National Sports Festival is in Jeju on Oct 16–22, so book flights and rooms early.

World Heritage Festival Jeju (세계유산축전)

Lava tube in Jeju · Photo: lumoplank, 2024 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: until Oct 18
  • Where: Geomunoreum lava tubes, Manjanggul, Seongsan Ilchulbong
  • Fee: programs need advance application
