Festivals in Seoul, Gyeonggi-do and Incheon for October 2026, checked as of October 12. The big first-week festivals are over, so this guide covers what is still ahead and lists the finished ones at the end. For the rest of Korea, see the October regional festivals post.

Dates come from official pages, Seoul’s festival portal, city notices and 2026 news. Check the official page for rain changes before you go. Photos are freely licensed pictures of past festivals or the venues, so this year may look different. Most city- and district-run festivals are free to enter, with paid activities and food.

Seoul

Mapo Naru Saeujeot Festival (마포나루 새우젓축제)

Saeujeot (salted shrimp) · Photo: 영길 황, 2018 · CC BY 2.0, Flickr

  • When: Oct 16–18 (16th 10:30–21:00, 17th 09:00–21:00, 18th 10:00–20:30)
  • Where: Peace Plaza, World Cup Park
  • Fee: free
  • Getting there: World Cup Stadium station (Line 6)

Highlights

  • Market for saeujeot (salted fermented shrimp, a kimchi essential) and regional produce
  • Re-enactment of shrimp boats arriving at the old Mapo port (Oct 16)
  • Closing fireworks and laser show, Oct 18 from 18:00

Gangdong Prehistoric Culture Festival (강동선사문화축제)

Pit houses at the Amsa-dong prehistoric site · Photo: Nakjibibimbap, 2025 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 16, 18:00–21:00; Oct 17–18, 10:00–21:00
  • Where: Seoul Amsa-dong Prehistoric Settlement Site
  • Fee: free
  • Getting there: Amsa History Park station (Line 8)

Highlights

  • Kids’ archaeology dig on a Neolithic village site
  • Opening drone show (Oct 16), closing fireworks (Oct 18)

Gwanak Gang Gam-chan Festival (관악강감찬축제)

Birthplace site of Gang Gam-chan, Nakseongdae · Photo: Boyhon, 2023 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 16–18
  • Where: Nakseongdae Park, birthplace of Goryeo general Gang Gam-chan
  • Getting there: Nakseongdae station (Line 2)

Highlights

  • Special show with aerial fireworks, Oct 17 at 19:00
  • Goryeo-themed food market

Seoul Silver Grass Festival (서울억새축제)

Silver grass at Haneul Park · Photo: Ji-soo Seo, 2015 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 17–23, 10:00–21:00
  • Where: Haneul Park, World Cup Park
  • Fee: free in past years
  • Getting there: World Cup Stadium or Mapo-gu Office station (Line 6), then a climb. Electric shuttle from Nanjicheon Park parking

Highlights

  • Silver grass (eoksae) at sunset, with the park open until 21:00
  • Grass bouquet making, busking

Nowon Moonlight Walk (노원달빛산책)

Danghyeoncheon stream · Photo: Ox1997cow, 2021 · CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 16–Nov 8, daily 17:30–22:00
  • Where: Danghyeoncheon stream near Junggye station (Line 7, exit 1)
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Light installations by 24 artist teams along a stream walk

Hangang Festival, Autumn (한강페스티벌 가을)

A 2025 water event at Ttukseom Hangang Park · Photo: Lee Jeong-woo, Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (Korea.net), 2025 · CC BY-SA 2.0, Flickr

  • When: Oct 17–25
  • Where: Han River parks, mainly Jamwon, Gangseo and Ttukseom
  • Official: https://seoul.go.kr/festa/hangang/

Highlights

  • Yachts, kayaks, tubing and cruises (booking needed)
  • Car-free Jamsugyo bridge, paper-airplane contest

Hanseong Baekje Cultural Festival (한성백제문화제)

Wildflower garden in Olympic Park · Photo: Lee Jun-young, Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (Korea.net), 2024 · CC BY-SA 2.0, Flickr

  • When: Oct 23–25
  • Where: 88 Lawn, Olympic Park
  • Getting there: Mongchontoseong station (Line 8) exit 1, or Hanseongbaekje station (Line 9) exit 2

Highlights

  • Baekje village and market on the site of the kingdom’s first capital
  • Concerts on Oct 23 and 25 at 19:00

Taste of Seoul (서울미식주간)

Dongdaemun Design Plaza · Photo: Dwxn, 2024 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 23–29
  • Where: DDP and restaurants and markets around Seoul
  • Official: https://tasteofseoul.visitseoul.net

Highlights

  • Special menus and discounts at Michelin-listed restaurants (book ahead)
  • Food market and market-tour cooking classes at DDP

Seoul BBQ Festival (서울 바비큐 페스티벌)

Nanji Hangang Park · Photo: JKD, 2022 · CC BY 2.0 kr, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 24–25, 12:00–20:30
  • Where: Nanji Hangang Park
  • Fee: free entry; meat is paid, self-BBQ zone needs a reservation
  • Getting there: shuttle from DMC station exit 5

Highlights

  • Self-BBQ zone and chef BBQ shows

Seafood Festival in the City (도심 속 바다축제)

Octopus at Noryangjin Fish Market · Photo: Basile Morin, 2024 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 24–25
  • Where: Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (Noryangjin station, Lines 1 and 9)

Highlights

  • Mock auction, shrimp catching, cooking demos

Jeongdong Night Walk (정동야행)

Chungdong First Methodist Church · Photo: 올리브 가지, 2023 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 30, 18:00–22:00; Oct 31, 14:00–22:00
  • Where: around Deoksugung Palace (City Hall station, Lines 1 and 2)
  • Fee: mostly free; some tours need online registration
  • Official: https://www.jeongdong-culturenight.kr

Highlights

  • Night openings of embassies and historic buildings in the old diplomatic quarter
  • Guided history tours

Gyeonggi-do

Icheon Rice Culture Festival (이천쌀문화축제)

Icheon rice · Photo: 최혜린, 2022 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 14–18
  • Where: Bokhacheon Waterfront Park, Icheon (new venue this year)
  • Official: https://www.ricefestival.or.kr

Highlights

  • New-crop Icheon rice sales and delivery
  • Food court

Myeongseongsan Silver Grass Festival (명성산 억새꽃축제)

  • When: Oct 16–18
  • Where: Sanjeong Lake and Myeongseongsan, Pocheon

Highlights

  • Ridge-top silver grass field (a real hike; wear hiking shoes)
  • Fireworks show on the lake

Paju Gaeseong Ginseng Festival (파주개성인삼축제)

Gyeongui Line steam locomotive at Imjingak · Photo: Gage Skidmore, 2025 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 17–18
  • Where: Imjingak Plaza near the DMZ (Imjingang station, Gyeongui-Jungang line)
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Six-year-old ginseng at 15–20% below market price
  • Ginseng bibimbap, outdoor films at night

Yeoju Ogok Naru Festival (여주오곡나루축제)

Silleuksa Temple, Yeoju · Photo: Altostratus, 2021 · CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 23–25
  • Where: Silleuksa Temple tourist area, Yeoju
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Giant-cauldron bibimbap and a royal tribute parade
  • Night performances on the river: tightrope walking and nakhwa fire-falling

Ansan Festa (안산페스타)

Hwarang Park amphitheater, Ansan · Photo: Aspere, 2023 · CC0, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: Oct 23–25
  • Where: Hwarang Park, Ansan

Highlights

  • Folk drama and ssireum wrestling themed on painter Kim Hong-do
  • Horse riding, tea ceremony and hanbok experiences

Haengjusanseong Fortress Heritage Night (행주산성 야행)

Chungjangsa shrine at Haengjusanseong · Photo: IRTC1015, 2016 · Public domain, Wikimedia Commons

  • When: until Oct 18, 18:00–22:00
  • Where: Haengjusanseong Fortress, Goyang

Highlights

  • Lit fortress walls and media art; concerts Fri–Sun at 18:30

Gyeonggi Ceramic Biennale (경기도자비엔날레)

Pottery from a Gyeonggi Ceramic Biennale workshop · Photo: USAG Humphreys, 2019 · CC BY 2.0, Flickr

  • When: until Nov 1
  • Where: Icheon, Gwangju and Yeoju
  • Fee: ₩11,000 combo ticket

Pairs well with the Icheon and Yeoju festivals.

Namhansanseong Autumn Festa (남한산성 가을 페스타)

Namhansanseong Fortress · Photo: xiquinhosilva, 2018 · CC BY 2.0, Flickr

  • When: until Oct 25
  • Where: Namhansanseong Fortress (UNESCO World Heritage)
  • Fee: free

Highlights

  • Traditional culture programs at the palace
  • Fortress-wall walk; autumn foliage peaks in late October

Incheon

Incheon’s big October festivals all finished in the first two weeks. The one left is the Subong Culture Festival at Subong Park on Oct 17 from 18:00.

War memorial at Subong Park · Photo: Ifflies, 2011 · CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
